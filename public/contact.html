> < a good bug report

What actually helps us fix it.

You don't need all of this. But each line you include roughly halves how long the fix takes, and the first two matter most.

  • Version and OS — the version is in the window title bar and on every release
  • What you did, in order — three steps beats a paragraph of description
  • What you expected, and what happened instead — both, even when it seems obvious
  • The model you were using — behaviour genuinely differs across the 340+
  • A screenshot — but check the top bar first: it shows the account you're signed in as

Ada is built by one person. Replies are fast on real problems and slower on wishlists — that's the honest trade, and it's why every route above is public.
