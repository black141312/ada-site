> < questions

Straight answers.

Is Free actually free, or a trial?
Actually free, with no time limit. Ada runs on your machine; with your own key there's nothing for us to meter. The repo map and semantic index run locally, so they cost nothing to operate either.
Does my code leave my machine?
Only what a turn sends to the model you chose — the same as any AI coding tool. Indexing is different: the embedding model runs on your machine, so your codebase is never uploaded to be indexed.
What happens if I stop paying for Pro?
The app keeps working. You drop to Free and add your own key. Nothing you built is locked up, because nothing you built lives with us.
Can we run the backend ourselves?
Yes — that's the Team tier's normal deployment. The backend is a container you run on your own infrastructure, with seats and OIDC pointing at your identity provider.
Ada is in beta. What does that mean for the price?
It ships, it's signed and notarized, and it updates itself. It's early, so access is still granted one account at a time — ask and we'll add you.
